# 3. Purpose of Department OUs

Department Organizational Units allow administrators to organize users according to the company's structure.

Examples:

- IT department
- Human Resources department
- Finance department
- Management department

They can later be used for:

- Group Policy Objects (GPO)
- Administrative delegation
- User organization
